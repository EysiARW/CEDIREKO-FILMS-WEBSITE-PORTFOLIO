About the studio

CEDIREKO is a one-person videography studio built on a simple rule: treat every subject the way you'd want to be treated on camera. From weddings and brand films to quiet everyday moments, the work leans on patient framing, honest color, and sound that breathes — never louder than the story itself. Every project starts with a conversation, not a checklist, because the best footage comes from people who feel comfortable enough to forget the camera is there.
