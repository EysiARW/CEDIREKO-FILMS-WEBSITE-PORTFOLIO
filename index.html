About CEDIREKO Films

CEDIREKO Films is an independent videography practice built on a simple rule: treat every subject the way you'd want to be treated on camera. Specializing in commercial brand narratives, live music performance films, and vertical editorial fashion series, the work focuses on patient framing, honest available light, and intentional pacing.

Every project begins with understanding the rhythm of the subject, creating space where people feel comfortable enough to forget the lens is there.
